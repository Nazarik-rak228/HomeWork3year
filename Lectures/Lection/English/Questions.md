In reality, education is extremely important because it equips people with knowledge and useful skills that they will need throughout their lives. However, one cannot claim that education alone guarantees a good job, since many other factors such as experience, connections and pure luck also play a significant role.

**2.**  
Overall, I believe my country currently has a rather outdated education system, and the general approach to teaching in schools is seriously flawed. Naturally, this situation is largely caused by widespread corruption and the extremely low salaries that teachers receive. Furthermore, higher education has become far too expensive for ordinary families and is also heavily affected by corruption. As a result, many talented students are forced either to study abroad or to give up the idea of getting a proper university degree.

Of course, education is by no means only for young people; learning should continue throughout a person’s entire life. In our country, however, economic realities mean that adults rarely have the opportunity to pursue full-scale formal education. This is mainly because it is extremely difficult to study and work full-time at the same time. Instead, older people usually acquire new knowledge through practical life experience or by learning specific skills that are directly connected to their jobs.

In my view, a good teacher should be patient, knowledgeable and friendly, and should be able to explain complex ideas in simple language. Moreover, a good teacher needs to understand that students learn at different paces and must be ready to help them whenever they encounter difficulties. It is also important that a teacher can motivate students and create a positive atmosphere in the classroom. Without these qualities, even the most knowledgeable person will struggle to teach effectively.

However, I think I would really enjoy teaching if I already had a primary online job and teaching were only a secondary activity for me. In that case, I could share knowledge without the constant pressure and low salary that most school teachers face. It would also give me the freedom to choose only the subjects I am truly passionate about.

**13.**  
As I have mentioned before, there are serious flaws in the school education system. It is worth noting that this outdated system really stifles children’s creativity because students are constantly expected to memorise information and give the single “correct” answer. Instead of encouraging original ideas, schools often punish any attempt to think differently, which is extremely damaging in the long run. I believe this approach produces obedient workers rather than independent thinkers.

**14.**  
Personally, I think tests and examinations are necessary because they allow teachers to check how well students understand a particular subject. However, I would not say that exams are always an accurate measure of someone’s knowledge. Some students perform badly under pressure even when they understand the material quite well, while others simply memorise answers without real comprehension. Therefore, exams should be used carefully and combined with other forms of assessment.

**15.**  
In my case, I learn best by combining theory with practical work, because simply reading or listening to information is not always enough for me to understand it properly. For instance, when I study programming, I usually need to write some code myself and make mistakes before I can really grasp how something works. This hands-on approach helps the knowledge stay in my memory much longer than pure theoretical study. I also find that explaining the material to someone else reinforces my own understanding.

**16.**  
Personally, I do not think that people need to have a similar level of education in order to spend time together comfortably. On the contrary, communicating with people who have different knowledge and life experience can be extremely useful. You can learn something new from them and discover completely different ways of looking at the world. In my opinion, diversity of education and background makes conversations much richer and more interesting.
